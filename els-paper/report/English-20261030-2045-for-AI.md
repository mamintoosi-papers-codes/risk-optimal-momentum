**Location:** `els-paper/cas-article.tex`, Proposition `prop:batch`, and its proof in the appendix.

\[

\[

=(1-\omega)\frac{\sigma_{t,j}^{2,(1)}}{B}

\]

\[
\mathbb{E}\!\left[
\max_j(\hat{\sigma}_{t,j}^{2}+\epsilon)
